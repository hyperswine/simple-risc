# Shifts complete in E1 and forward from E2

Kind: core change, simulation pricing and timing builds, 2026-11-05. Applies
to HEAD 5bae919 (E2->D forwarding, fprisc-81 at placement 1). Follows
[NEXT-STEPS](2026-11-02-NEXT-STEPS.md) and the hazard split in
[STALL-SPLIT](2026-10-30-STALL-SPLIT.md).

## Result

Shifts were the largest hazard producer left after E2->D forwarding: on the
frozen 128/64 classifier, dependents waiting on a shift cost **10.4% of all
cycles** (E1 3.2%, E2 3.7%, M 3.4%), because E1 did only the low two levels,
the high three happened at commit in M, and decode stalled through all three
stages.

The whole shift now completes in E1 from its registered operands, so a shift
forwards from E2 like any other ALU result and M has nothing to finish.
Cycles fall **1.041x** (103.52M to 99.47M, IPC 0.382 to 0.397) with the
classifier output unchanged, and the build at 81 MHz closes with *more*
margin than before: 82.65 MHz, +0.247 ns, against 81.24 MHz, +0.036 ns.

## Pricing

Two options were implemented and simulated (frozen classifier, 48/48 MHz,
`--split`):

| Variant | Cycles | Speedup | IPC | Shift stalls E1 / E2 / M |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| HEAD (two-stage shift) | 103,519,746 | 1.000 | 0.382 | 3.34M / 3.87M / 3.53M |
| V1: forward the finished shift from M | 101,077,782 | 1.024 | 0.391 | 3.33M / 3.87M / 0 |
| V2: whole shift in E1 (chosen) | 99,472,799 | 1.041 | 0.397 | 3.26M / 0.02M / 0 |

V2's residual E2 shift stalls are shifts whose E2 action is not `Plain`
(they never forward). The E1 stall is the same one every dependent of a
plain result pays.

A chained-shift program (`prop_pipelined_shifts_forward_from_e2`, eight
dependents of shifts) takes 31 cycles instead of 43.

## What changed

- E1's `other1` shifts by all five amount bits (`op2 .&. 31`) instead of two.
  E1's inputs (`e1A`, `e1Op2`) are registers, so the five-level shifter sits
  between registers next to the 32-bit adder.
- `e2Forwards` no longer excludes shifts; `mFinal` no longer waits for them.
- `committedValue` only distinguishes loads. The `mShift`, `mArith`,
  `mAmount` and `e2ShiftFast` fields are gone (they were constant after the
  change). The multi-cycle phase machine keeps its own two-stage
  `ShiftFinish` path untouched.
- Test: `prop_pipelined_shifts_forward_from_e2` pins results and cycles.

## Timing

CPU 81 / SDRAM 48, the fprisc-81 options, placement 1:

| Design | Fmax | Worst setup slack |
|---|---:|---:|
| HEAD 5bae919 | 81.238 MHz | +0.036 ns |
| V2 (pricing build, before the field cleanup) | 82.651 MHz | +0.247 ns |

The full shifter does not appear among the 25 tightest paths; the limiters
are the M->D hazard check into `pDBlocked` (+0.247) and D's word into E1's
operand (+0.330), both older than this change. Routing noise between two
placements of different netlists is part of the +0.211 ns, so the fair
reading is "no worse", not "better".

## Board

The `fprisc-81` preset rebuilt from the final source (82.651 MHz, +0.247 ns,
manifest ready) was loaded into FPGA SRAM. `check_processor.py`,
`check_rv32m.py` (30,720) and `check_rv32i.py` (38,400) pass at 81 MHz, and
the FP-RISC suite passes 7/7 through the official runner
(`--preset fprisc-81`).

Classifier, 512/128 rows, ten runs, median per run:

| Image | Binary | Per run |
|---|---|---:|
| E2D image (5bae919) | `93e027cc` | 7.338 s |
| This image | `93e027cc` (same binary) | **7.097 s** (-3.3%) |
| This image | today's FP-RISC build, `8ade8a13` | 7.156 s |

The board saving is smaller than the simulated 4.1% because it is a
different workload at a different clock ratio: the 512/128 classifier at
CPU 81 / SDRAM 48, where cache-miss holds are a larger share of the time
than in the frozen 128/64 classifier simulated at 48/48, so a hazard saving
buys less. The same held for E2->D forwarding (4.5% on the board against
7.3% simulated). FP-RISC's compiler moved between the two runs (a7f4fd6 and
later), so the same-binary row is the hardware comparison.
