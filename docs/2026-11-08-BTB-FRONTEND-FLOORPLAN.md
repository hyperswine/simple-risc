# Census-sized BTB/front-end placement sweep

Status: complete, 2026-11-08. Tests the physical-locality hypothesis from
[LOOP-CENSUS](2026-11-08-LOOP-CENSUS.md) using the revised comparison method
in [ARCHITECTURAL-REFACTOR](2026-11-07-ARCHITECTURAL-REFACTOR.md).

## Frozen experiment

Both arms use the identical frozen `dcd220d` synthesized netlist and ROM from
`output/refactor-2026-11-07/baseline-physical`. No aggregate-area changes or
new RTL are combined with this experiment. Baseline regenerates the canonical
floorplan and asserts that its groups exactly match the earlier constraints.
Candidate is generated by `frontend_floorplan.py`.

Each arm runs all 15 combinations of placement options 0–4 and requested frequency options
87, 89, 91 MHz. Timing constraints stay CPU/cache 81 MHz, memory 48 MHz;
map 3, route 1, route fanout 8 remain fixed. Every completed route requests
3,000 setup paths and hard-block locations. Three jobs run concurrently,
with a fixed 1,800-second route limit per tool attempt. License failures may
retry; elapsed time is not a controlled router-speed measurement.

The candidate is fixed before collecting results. No settings are selected
based on intermediate timing. Pass count uses all 15 settings. Medians over
completed timing reports are labelled conditional; missing results get explicit
bounds for the full-grid median rather than being silently discarded. These
are deterministic configurations, not independent random samples.

## Candidate construction

The measured baseline packing is 15,170 routed logic units / 8,717 occupied
CLS = 1.7403 units/CLS. The census attributes logic to the registers it feeds,
splitting shared logic across fields. Required capacity is
`ceil(1.5 * max(attributed_logic / packing, selected_registers / 2))`.
Slow groups are charged their entire fields even when coupled registers are
excluded. The 50% allowance is a sizing heuristic, not a routability guarantee.

| Group | Selected registers | Attributed logic | Required CLS | Region | Estimated capacity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| CSR/counters | 437 | 1,888 | 1,628 | R[2:9]C[2:55] | 1,728 |
| Bus/devices | 284 | 1,260 | 1,086 | R[36:53]C[2:19] | 1,152 |
| Fetch/A/F/BTB | 1,150 | 3,318 | 2,860 | R[17:36]C[16:55] | 2,880 |

The generator chooses the smallest row band meeting the capacity around fixed
anchors, with fixed column bands. Capacity assumes four CLS per tile and
conservatively excludes entire RAM/DSP rows 10, 19, 28, 37, 46. This is not
an exact device resource map. Groups are non-exclusive; the bus and front
rectangles overlap at one row/four columns. Other logic remains placeable
inside the rectangles. Register constraints encourage, but do not force,
their feeding LUTs to follow.

The two BTB halves are pinned to adjacent legal sites `BSRAM_R28[12]` and
`BSRAM_R28[13]`, replacing their separation across rows in the baseline.
Cache and register-file RAM sites remain free. The usual one-cycle boundary
exclusion applies to slow groups only, preserving the pipeline-coupled CSR
and bus registers' freedom. Front-end state is deliberately constrained.

A separate union-of-feeding-cones check finds 3,376 LUT equivalents for the
selected front registers, versus 3,318 attributed by the census; selected
CSR and bus cones are 1,637 and 1,189 respectively. Thus the front estimate
is not hiding a large shared-logic undercount. This check does not measure
physical congestion or prove that the geometry is suitable.

This tests the combined strategy: front-end grouping, BTB anchoring, and
census-sized slow regions. It cannot isolate their individual contributions.

## Preflight

The first proposed pair used `R28[13]` and `R28[14]`; Gowin rejected index 14
before placement. The corrected pair passes constraint parsing. Aborted
preflight/restart outputs are isolated from the measured sweep and excluded
from all statistics. The driver holds a filesystem lock to prevent duplicate
sweep processes. Final input hashes are recorded in `protocol.json`.

## Frequency-option limitation

The first baseline comparisons at requested 87 and 89 MHz give identical
hard-block sites and all 3,000 parsed timing paths for placements 3 and 0.
The installed Gowin `data/config/synthesisoptions.xml` identifies `-frequency`
as a synthesis option. This experiment skips synthesis and keeps explicit
81/48 MHz SDC constraints. Varying `-frequency` and `-global_freq` therefore
must not be assumed to produce distinct placer targets. The full requested
grid is still recorded; final summaries also count distinct fingerprints of
the reported timing paths and hard-block sites. Equal fingerprints establish
equal reported outcomes, not necessarily every unreported wire being equal.

## Interpretation and decision

The candidate produced **no completed routed timing report**. Its pass count
is zero, and neither its Fmax distribution nor BTB/front-end route-delay
improvement can be measured from these attempts. The baseline has working
implementations under the same conditions. **Do not adopt this candidate.**

This rejects the tested combination of region geometry, register grouping
and BTB anchors under this budget. It does not establish that locality is
unhelpful, or identify which of those three choices caused the failures.
The constraints bind registers and two RAMs; they do not explicitly bind
all feeding LUTs. The 50% area allowance is not a routing-capacity guarantee.

The frequency-option repetitions did not add distinct baseline timing/site
outcomes in the completed comparisons. Treat the five placement options as
explicit configurations, and do not describe the requested 15 rows as 15
independent physical samples. Future target sweeps need a verified PNR
control that actually changes implementation while preserving an 81 MHz
acceptance check; synthesis-frequency options on a frozen netlist are not
established as that control.

Synthesis area and logical fanout are identical by construction. In the
unrouted placement-4 reports, candidate occupied CLS is 8,610 versus baseline
8,629, but unrouted nets rise from 557 to 3,454. Those packing counts belong
to incomplete routes and do not constitute a usable area win.

The next diagnostic would separate BTB anchoring from front-end grouping
and relax the region geometry, measuring each constraint contribution.
No further candidate is implemented or swept in this experiment.

## Validation and evidence

Baseline constraints regenerated identically; production netlist and ROM
hashes were shared by both arms. Generated instance membership was checked
for existence and duplicates. Gowin accepted the corrected constraints.
The existing baseline timing values reproduce exactly. No RTL changed, so
no new functional behavior or simulation qualification is claimed. No board
was programmed; canonical presets and floorplan remain unchanged.

[Reproduction scripts, constraints, receipts and summaries](evidence/2026-11-08-btb-floorplan/)
are retained with a hash manifest. Full 3,000-path reports remain under
`output/btb-floorplan-2026-11-08/`, identified by per-run timing hashes.
All changes remain local and uncommitted.
