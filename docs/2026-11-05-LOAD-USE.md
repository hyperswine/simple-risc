# Load-use: large in simulation, not placeable at 81 MHz

Kind: pricing and timing experiment, 2026-11-05. Applies to HEAD 187c637
(shifts in E1). Nothing is adopted: the core is unchanged.

## Result

Loads were the next hazard producer, at about 14.8% of classifier cycles:
9.9% of issue slots lost behind a load waiting in M, 3.6% of dependents
waiting on a load in M, and 1.3% on a load in E1/E2. A load waits three
cycles in M because the cache port answers four clocks after E2 issues the
read; the fourth clock (`cpu_rdata_return`, 1679292) only repeats
`cpu_word` to split the cache-to-core route.

Two changes priced well in simulation, but neither places at the 81 MHz
canon, so neither is adopted:

| Variant | Cycles | Speedup | IPC | Timing |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| HEAD 187c637 | 99,472,799 | 1.000 | 0.397 | 81 MHz, +0.247 ns |
| L1: decode takes a load's word in its commit cycle | 98,478,670 | 1.010 | 0.401 | 81 MHz p1: 62.7 MHz |
| L2: three-clock cache port | 89,927,551 | 1.106 | 0.439 | 81 MHz p3/p1: 61.9 / 69.6 MHz |
| L1 + L2 | 88,994,215 | 1.118 | 0.444 | 81 MHz: best 78.9 MHz (p3); 78 MHz: passes p3 only, +0.173 ns |

Classifier output was correct in every simulation (train 124/128 digest
21562, test 61/64 digest 909572).

## Why L2 was worth pricing

L2 removes a cycle from every load in M and from every fetch after a
redirect: with it the "behind a load in M" share falls from 10.3% to 7.4%
and each restart's refill from 7.56 to 6.38 cycles. Functionally it is
sound: the Haskell suite, both cache regressions (bench at three clocks) and
the Verilator CPU check pass with L1 + L2.

## Why it does not place

Without the return register the placer must put the core beside the cache,
and failures spread across every structure (front end, machine state, BTB,
E1/E2) at 79-88% routing. L1 alone, a single extra operand-mux input, sent
placement 1 from 82.65 MHz to 62.7 MHz, with every failing path in A -> F
and the BTB, logic L1 does not touch. The design is at the edge of
routability: small changes move whole structures across the chip.

With higher CPU clocks the goal and 81 MHz the canon
([SDRAM-CLOCK-DECISION](2026-11-05-SDRAM-CLOCK-DECISION.md)), L1 + L2 at
78 MHz (1.075x in clock x IPC, on one fragile placement) is not taken.

## What would unlock it

- Floorplanning: a region constraint keeping the cache and the core's
  memory-facing stages together, so the three-clock route is short by
  construction (`SDRAM_REGION_CST` exists). This is the congestion work in
  [NEXT-STEPS](2026-11-02-NEXT-STEPS.md) item 4, and it would also be the way
  to stabilise placement generally.
- Then re-price L2 first (it carries 1.106x of the 1.118x).
